Our Story

From humble beginnings in Kerala's spice farms to becoming a trusted global supplier of premium natural spices

Welcome to Ellakai Spices

Founded in 2010, Ellakai Spices began with a simple mission: to bring the authentic flavors of India's finest spices directly from farmers to kitchens worldwide. What started as a small family business in Kerala has grown into a trusted wholesale supplier serving restaurants, hotels, and supermarkets across 50+ countries.

We believe in transparency, quality, and sustainability. Every spice we sell is handpicked from farms we personally visit, ensuring you receive only the freshest, most aromatic products. By eliminating middlemen, we guarantee fair prices for farmers and unmatched quality for our customers.

Today, we partner with over 500 farmers across Kerala, Karnataka, and Tamil Nadu, supporting sustainable farming practices while delivering premium spices that elevate every dish they touch.
