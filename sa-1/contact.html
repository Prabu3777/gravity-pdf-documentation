FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for wholesale?

Our minimum wholesale order is 50kg. We offer competitive pricing for bulk orders starting from 50kg and above.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by location.

Are your spices organic and certified?

Yes, all our spices are 100% natural and certified by FSSAI. We also have ISO 9001:2015 certification and provide lab test reports.

How long does delivery take?

Domestic orders typically take 5-7 business days. International orders take 10-15 business days depending on the destination.

Can I visit your facility or farms?

Yes! We welcome visits from wholesale clients. Please contact us in advance to schedule a tour of our facilities and partner farms.
